## Active objective

Establish the canonical Himsat product thesis, constraints, researched gap analysis, architecture, donor/provenance rules, security/platform truth, qualification framework, and dependency-ordered execution program before any donor-code transplant or product implementation begins.

## Why implementation is intentionally not yet authorized

The repository began empty on 2026-09-06. The project has a large ambition spanning sensitive audio capture, mobile background execution, local AI, encrypted memory, document parsing, cross-device sync, and agent/plugin capabilities. Starting implementation before the following boundaries are explicit would create avoidable licensing, security, portability, and architectural debt.

Specification 000 therefore owns only the planning/governance foundation.

## Required Specification 000 closeout conditions

Specification 000 may be considered ready for closeout only when the planning branch proves all of the following against its exact head:

1. canonical product constitution exists;
2. researched landscape/gap analysis exists and distinguishes source facts from product proposals;
3. platform capture claims are version-gated and do not promise unsupported phone/system/call capture;
4. architecture defines a Rust-first core plus native mobile boundaries;
5. document intelligence, portable AI context, background recording, Himsat Bridge, memory/evidence, agents, and sync are represented in the product architecture;
6. donor policy distinguishes `COPY`, `DEPEND`, `VENDOR`, and `REFERENCE` and records incompatible/mixed-license examples;
7. security threat model includes stolen device, malicious plugin/model/document, prompt injection, compromised storage, pairing attacks, crash/power/storage failure, and connector exfiltration;
8. qualification strategy defines speech, diarization, retrieval, document parsing, publishing, privacy, reliability, battery/thermal, and evidence metrics;
9. execution master plan decomposes implementation into dependency-ordered bounded units;
10. no product implementation or donor code is smuggled into the foundation planning change;
11. exact changed paths are reviewed and planning consistency checks are performed;
12. residual unknowns and deferred research are recorded rather than silently converted to assumptions.
